## UK FIG Regime
- Foreign Income and Gains: 0% tax on foreign gains for first 4 years of UK residency
- Requires 10+ years non-UK residence prior (Sabine qualifies — never lived in UK)
- Started April 2025
- CRITICAL: foreign capital LOSSES are not deductible under FIG — all losing positions must be sold before becoming UK tax resident
- Regime could be scrapped by future UK government — monitor
